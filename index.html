VEGETATION DENSITY OF INDIAN SUNDARBAN

📄 ABSTRACT
The Indian Sundarban, a UNESCO World Heritage site, hosts one of the world's largest mangrove ecosystems. Vegetation density is a critical indicator of forest health, carbon stock, and biodiversity. This study analyses multi‑temporal satellite-derived vegetation indices (NDVI, EVI) to map density changes from 1991 to 2022.
